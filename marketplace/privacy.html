Marketplace Privacy & Identity Checks

Last updated: October 13, 2026. This document explains the identity data used for account security and seller/buyer anti-fraud checks.

What is collected

Marketplace account credentials (password stored as a hash), the Discord user ID you link through our Discord bot, verified WhatsApp number, verified Bedrock XUID and/or Java UUID and Minecraft username, seller QR recipient details, trade and dispute records. Your full WhatsApp number is not published on seller profiles.

Why we require verification

Discord verification proves control of the linked Discord account. WhatsApp OTP proves access to the phone number at the time of verification. Minecraft linking requires server-supplied authenticated identity, not a typed IGN. All three are required before trading to reduce identity fraud, fake transactions, and review abuse. Verification does not eliminate every scam or prove a payment was made.

WhatsApp verification provider

OTP delivery and validation require a real WhatsApp verification provider, configured by the owner. Sending an OTP transmits your submitted phone number to that provider solely for verification. You must request the OTP yourself. Never give an OTP to staff or another player.

Account linking and requests

Bound Discord IDs, WhatsApp numbers and Minecraft identities cannot be replaced by players through the website. If you need identity recovery, privacy support, correction or lawful account deletion, contact the official AbszRPG Discord moderators. Moderator recovery requires evidence and an audit. We retain transaction evidence only as needed for fraud prevention, disputes and applicable legal obligations.

QR payments

For RM marketplace listings, an owner-reviewed seller QR and recipient name are disclosed to the authenticated buyer only after an order enters the active trading stage. Funds go directly to the seller's bank/e-wallet. The website does not hold, insure, escrow or reverse QR transfers. Store bank evidence privately and report any suspicious transaction to official staff.

Young players

Players who need parental or guardian consent under applicable law should obtain it before submitting their phone number or paying sellers.

All marketplace transactions remain subject to AbszRPG rules, Minecraft usage guidelines and applicable privacy and payment laws.
