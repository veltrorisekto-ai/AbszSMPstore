TRADE SMART, TRADE SAFER

Marketplace Safety Rules

This site tracks orders, not actual Minecraft inventory or payments. Player usernames and Discord IDs are self-reported unless staff explicitly verifies them. A high rating does not guarantee a risk-free trade.

  1. Verify the server and item.

    Confirm the edition, exact quantity, condition and agreed price before you make a trade.

  2. Use only official staff for midman trades.

    Wait for approval and independently confirm the staff member inside the official Discord server. No impersonators.

  3. No early payment or delivery.

    Never send money or valuables based on a screenshot, unverified message, or claim that escrow has been funded.

  4. Protect your account.

    Never reveal Minecraft passwords, one-time codes, Discord recovery codes, or account access.

  5. Keep evidence and report disputes.

    Save trade details, order ID, and Discord messages. Use Dispute if a live order has a problem.

  6. Review after successful receipt.

    Only the buyer can press Order Received after the seller confirms delivery. Honest reviews cannot be bought or traded.

Player-to-player sales must comply with AbszoluteCraft server rules, Minecraft/Mojang usage policies, and applicable laws. Staff-supervised means staff coordination, not a payment guarantee or automatic escrow. Off-site Discord payments and trades remain the responsibility of participants.
