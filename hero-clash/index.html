FROM DOWNLOAD TO DUNGEON

Your first steps

Keep your account recovery code somewhere private.

  1. Install the official APK

    Download this release and open the APK on your Android device. Follow Android's installation and security checks.

  2. Download & verify resources

    Open the app while online and approve the resource download. Interrupted downloads can resume.

  3. Create an account or log in

    Choose a username and a password of at least 12 characters. Existing players can use the same game account.

  4. Build your party

    Invite another hero. The party leader enters a dungeon first, then selects “Summon Party Here”. Both players need the current version.

BEFORE YOU SET OUT

Good to know

I already have the Design Tester. What should I do?

This public app installs separately from Hero Clash Design Tester. Log in once with the same username and password to load your confirmed server progress. The new app downloads its own resource copy. Keep the tester installed until you have confirmed your account in the public app and any pending actions have synced.

Are paid diamond top-ups available?

No. Real-money checkout is disabled in this beta. Use the gems and coins you earn in the game.

What if Google Play Protect shows a warning?

Google may scan or block unfamiliar apps. This APK has a verified release signature, but that is not Google approval and does not guarantee that a warning will disappear. If installation is blocked, share the exact message with official support so we can investigate. Play Protect review has not yet been completed.

Can I play offline?

No. Resource checks, account login and online gameplay require an internet connection. If your connection drops, the app retries and reloads confirmed server progress.

What should I include in a bug report?

Tell us your app version, Android version, phone model, what happened and the steps that caused it. A screenshot helps. Never include your password or recovery code. Contact the official Discord community below.

Where are the asset credits and licences?

Third-party model, image and audio notices supplied with the game are retained in the downloadable resource pack. Promotional art on this page is not a gameplay screenshot.
