MY HERO CLASH WITH MONSTER

Privacy notice

Effective 26 October 2026. This notice describes the Android public beta operated by ABSZ Projects & Studios. It applies to this game; other ABSZ services have their own notices.

Account and game information

We store your username, a salted password hash, a hashed recovery code, session records and your game progress. Progress includes heroes, inventory, currency, quests and dungeon rewards. Your username and hero details can be visible to other players. Messages you send in the shared chat are visible to other players in that world.

Login on your device

The Android app stores your remembered login token using Android Keystore-backed encryption. It does not store your password. Resources and pending game actions are cached on your device. An unfinished account-recovery screen may temporarily retain its recovery code in that encrypted local session until you confirm it has been saved.

Service operation and analytics

Our Cloudflare-hosted services process network requests and store account and gameplay records. We use hashed network identifiers and short-lived counters to limit abusive authentication requests. The game sends gameplay events, onboarding milestones and error diagnostics to help investigate bugs and improve the beta. Telemetry records are scheduled for removal after 30 days when the server's event cleanup runs. Hosting providers may process network metadata under their own policies.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate players, remember sessions, save progress, deliver resources, synchronize multiplayer, prevent duplicate rewards, limit abuse and diagnose problems. This beta has no advertising features or active real-money checkout.

Retention and your choices

Account and game records are retained to operate the service and support account recovery. Signing out revokes that login session. You can request access, correction or deletion through the official ABSZ Discord moderators; we may need to verify account ownership before acting. Limited records may be retained where required for security, dispute handling or applicable obligations.

Support and privacy requests

Contact the official ABSZ community and request a private conversation with a moderator. Never post your password or recovery code publicly.

Changes to this beta

We will update this notice if the game's data handling changes, including before enabling payments or additional sign-in providers.
