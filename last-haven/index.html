How do squads and maps work?

Each co-op match supports up to 10 players on its own map. Public matchmaking creates another match when the current one is full. Use a private room code to invite your friends.

Where is my progress saved?

Co-op progress is saved by the game server and linked to this installation’s device identity. Solo progress is stored on your device. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove that identity and local saves. Cross-device account login is not available in this beta.

What is included in this beta?

Solo survival and a 10-player co-op mode with shared loot, barricades, combat, revives and squad text chat. Some advanced solo features are not yet available in co-op. Internet is required for multiplayer and launcher updates.

How do updates work?

The PC launcher verifies signed update information and checks downloaded game files before opening them. Android updates are installed from a new APK on this official page; install over the existing app to keep its data.

What should I do if something goes wrong?

Keep your device and System WebView current. On PC, extract the complete archive to a writable folder and keep the included files together. Report the platform, steps and any error message in our community.
