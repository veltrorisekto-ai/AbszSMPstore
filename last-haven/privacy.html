LAST HAVEN · PUBLIC BETA

Privacy & game data

Updated 29 October 2026

Data on your device

LAST HAVEN stores solo saves, preferences and a randomly generated multiplayer device identity in app storage. The Windows launcher also stores verified game updates and settings locally. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove your identity and local progress.

Online play

Multiplayer uses Cloudflare to process your chosen player name, appearance, actions, squad text messages, room membership and saved co-op progress. The server stores a hash of the device authentication token. Network providers may process technical request information such as IP addresses to operate and protect the service. Do not put private information in your player name or squad chat.

Permissions & downloads

The Android app requests internet access. It does not request contacts, microphone, camera or location permissions. Windows updates connect to the official LAST HAVEN asset service and verify signed release information and file checksums. This beta has no account login or purchase system.

Support

For support or a request concerning your game data, contact ABSZ Projects & Studios through our community. Include your player ID when available; never share your device token or private credentials.
