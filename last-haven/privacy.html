LAST HAVEN · PUBLIC BETA

Privacy & game data

Updated 29 October 2026

Data on your device

LAST HAVEN stores solo saves, preferences and a randomly generated multiplayer device identity in app storage. The Windows launcher also stores verified game updates and settings locally. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove guest access and local practice progress. Register and keep your login credentials to recover your online wallet. Automatic password recovery is not available in this beta.

Online play

Multiplayer uses Cloudflare to process your chosen player name, appearance, actions, squad text messages, room membership and saved co-op progress. The server stores a hash of the device authentication token. Network providers may process technical request information such as IP addresses to operate and protect the service. Other survivors in the shared lobby can see your name, appearance, movement and overhead chat bubbles. Do not put private information in your player name, lobby chat or squad chat.

Permissions & downloads

The Android app requests internet access and asks for microphone permission when you turn on voice chat. Voice is relayed to players in your selected lobby or squad; it is not intentionally recorded or saved by the game. You can turn off your microphone or mute playback. The app does not request contacts, camera or location permissions. Windows updates connect to the official LAST HAVEN asset service and verify signed release information and file checksums. You can create a survivor account with a username and password to recover online progress on another device. The server stores a salted password verifier, session token hashes, your online credit balance, owned classes and credit transaction history. Credits and class rolls are validated on the server. Top-ups are available through manual Touch ’n Go / DuitNow payment verification. Order and payment reference records are retained for payment review, fulfilment and support; a submitted reference alone does not award credits. Lobby survivors can send you short-lived squad invitations; only the invited player receives the invitation.

Support

For support or a request concerning your game data, contact ABSZ Projects & Studios through our community. Include your player ID when available; never share your device token or private credentials.
