? Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the game free?⌄

The official Online Alpha build is available to download. The game is still in development and may contain bugs.

Can I play on Android and PC?⌄

Yes. Android and Windows connect to the same multiplayer game server. Guest sessions are separate on each device, and cross-save is not yet available. Aegis is a separate realm; existing Dawnspire characters stay in Dawnspire Legacy.

Why does Windows Defender show a warning?⌄

The alpha launcher does not yet have an Authenticode certificate. Download only from the official website, verify its SHA-256 checksum, and keep your antivirus protection enabled.

Where are my existing characters?⌄

Aegis (Malaysia Community) is a new realm with separate characters. Existing characters stay in Dawnspire Legacy, whose service recovery is pending. Changing servers does not move characters between realms.

What if my download fails?⌄

Try again with a stable connection and make sure your device has enough free storage. If it still fails, report the issue on Discord with your device details and the error message.

Do I need a new APK or ZIP for every update?⌄

Compatible content can update in-game. Changes to the launcher, engine, or native components still require a new installer.
